Hello! I am Tonmoy. I am a Research Fellow at Massachusetts General Hospital and Harvard Medical School.

I specialize in developing advanced optical imaging systems, image analysis tools, and AI algorithms for translational research. My expertise is centered on polarization-sensitive optical coherence tomography (PS-OCT) and optical frequency-domain imaging (OFDI).

My professional background is supported by a robust technical skill set that includes optical system design and implementation, signal and image processing. I have a strong foundation in machine learning and computer vision.

Beyond research, I have cultivated leadership skills as the Vice President of the UCR Photonics Society, where I co-led initiatives to foster a collaborative community. I am passionate about applying my technical and analytical skills in an innovative healthcare or biomedical technology setting to drive impactful scientific discoveries.
